Join us as we shape the future of live music.

MusicCred is a platform that connects artists, venues, and fans, built to simplify and streamline the live music booking process.

With a commitment to empowering independent artists and small to medium-sized venues, MusicCred provides the tools and technology needed to succeed in the ever-evolving music industry. In short, and as our company motto suggests, MusicCred is “getting music back to business.”

The app ushers in a new era in the live music industry by simplifying the process for venues to connect with reputable artists, offering artists a seamless way to book shows and engage with their fanbase. Venue and artist negotiations are streamlined as terms can be discussed directly within the app, while payments for shows are facilitated securely through the app as well. Bookings made through the app become live public events for fans looking for live entertainment, and MusicCred’s geo-location technology brings fans closer than ever to the music they love.

MusicCred’s mission is to bring people closer through live music. Its hallmark is inclusivity: welcoming artists and venues of diverse genres and sizes, and promoting a broadened musical horizon for all.

Founder

Chad R. LaBoy, Founder & CEO. Hailing from Florida, Chad combines the drive of an entrepreneur with the vision of a philanthropist. Since founding MusicCred in 2018 he has built the company around a simple idea: that the people who make live music and the places that host it deserve tools as good as any other industry’s. Beyond MusicCred, he introduced the concept of “CoOpetition,” a collaborative approach to modern commerce. Under his leadership MusicCred is not merely observing industry shifts but working to lead them.
