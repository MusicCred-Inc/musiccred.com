How it works

Much like ride-sharing and home-sharing reshaped their industries, MusicCred reshapes how live entertainment is booked and how people connect with the artists they love.

IdentityVenues and artists create, manage and control their MusicCred page.
ConnectionWe make it easy for venues and artists to find the right fit.
InteractionEither side can request a show; terms are negotiated inside the app.
CredibilityCredibility is earned and leveraged, which means more secure bookings.

Built for all three sides of live music

One platform for the people who make the show, the people who host it, and the people who come to see it.

For artists

Book more, chase less

Customize your page with photos, video and music, request shows at venues, negotiate terms in the app, get paid through the app, and keep your bandmates and bookings in sync.

For venues

Fill the calendar with credible talent

Book artists, showcase your lineup and hours, find last-minute replacements, pay for shows seamlessly, and reach fans directly with specials and perks.

For fans

Find live music near you

Fan View uses your location to surface live entertainment around you and connects you with the artists and venues you love.
