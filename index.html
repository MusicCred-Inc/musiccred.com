How it works

Booking a show should take a swipe and a tap.

Much like ride-sharing and home-sharing reshaped their industries, MusicCred reshapes how live entertainment is booked and how people connect with the artists they love.

Identity

Venues and artists create, manage and control their own MusicCred page: photos, video, music, hours, lineup.

Connection

Search by location, genre and availability. We make it easy for venues and artists to find the right fit.

Interaction

Either side can request a show. Terms are discussed and agreed inside the app, and payment runs through it.

Credibility

Credibility is earned and leveraged on the platform, which means more secure bookings for everyone.

Built for all three sides of live music

For the people who make the show, the people who host it,
and the people who come to see it.

For venues

Discover & book talent

Browse artist pages with photos, video and music, then send a booking request or an inquiry in one tap. No cold calls, no email chains.

  • Verified artist pages with bio, media and social links
  • Request to book, or send an inquiry first
  • Find last-minute replacements when a slot opens
For artists & venues

Manage bookings and get paid

Inquiries, confirmations, balances and payouts live in one inbox. Everyone sees the same status, and payment for the show runs securely through the app.

  • Pending, accepted, balance due, payout complete, at a glance
  • Keep bandmates and calendars in sync
  • Negotiate terms in the thread, not over the phone
For fans

Find the show, right where you are

Every confirmed booking becomes a public, shareable live event. Fan View uses your location to surface live music around you tonight, this weekend, or when you travel.

  • Geo-targeted map of events, venues and artists
  • Follow the artists and venues you love
  • More customers for venues, more exposure for artists

MusicCred Cares

Live music that gives back.
